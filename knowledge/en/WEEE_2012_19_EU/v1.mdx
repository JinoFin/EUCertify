# WEEE 2012/19/EU – Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment

## Meaning
Obliges producers to organise collection, treatment and recycling of waste EEE placed on the EU market.

## Scope
- All categories in Annex III (household, IT, lighting, medical etc.)
- Applies to distance sellers and manufacturers without EU presence via authorised representatives

## When it applies
Whenever EEE is placed on a Member State market. Registration is required in each country.

## Obligations by role
- **Manufacturer/Importer:** Register with national WEEE schemes, report quantities, finance take-back, apply crossed-out wheeled bin symbol
- **EU authorised representative:** Act on behalf of non-EU producers for registration and compliance

## Conformity assessment
No modular process; compliance demonstrated through national registrations and reporting.

## Documentation
- National registration certificates
- Quantity and recycling reports
- Contracts with collection and treatment partners

## Marking
- Crossed-out wheeled bin symbol (with bar for products placed after 2005)
- User instructions covering separate collection

## Retention & traceability
Keep registration and reporting data for at least 5 years. Record serial numbers/batches for recall coordination.

## Note
Check national specifics (e.g. German guarantee filings, B2C vs B2B differentiation).
