# EMC 2014/30/EU – Electromagnetic Compatibility

## Meaning
Ensures devices limit electromagnetic emissions and remain immune to disturbances from other equipment.

## Scope
- Electrical/electronic devices, systems and fixed installations
- Prototypes, custom-built and series products
- Excludes radio equipment covered entirely by RED, aeronautical products, passive components

## When it applies
Whenever a device may emit or be affected by conducted or radiated interference and is not exclusively governed by RED.

## Obligations by role
- **Manufacturer:** Perform EMC assessment, compile test reports, maintain technical documentation, apply CE mark
- **Importer:** Confirm documentation, ensure series products meet EMC requirements
- **EU authorised representative:** Keep declaration and support authorities

## Conformity assessment
Module A with harmonised standards such as EN 55032/55035 or EN 61000 series. Without standards provide engineering justification or involve a notified body.

## Documentation
- EMC emission and immunity reports
- Test setups and configurations
- Disturbance risk analysis
- EU Declaration of Conformity (EMC)

## Marking
- CE mark
- User instructions covering EMC-relevant installation and usage conditions

## Retention & traceability
Store technical file for 10 years. Document serial numbers and EMC test configurations.

## Note
For fixed installations keep EMC documentation at the installation site.
