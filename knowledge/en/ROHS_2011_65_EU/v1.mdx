# RoHS 2011/65/EU – Restriction of Hazardous Substances

## Meaning
Limits lead, cadmium, mercury, hexavalent chromium, PBB, PBDE and certain phthalates in electrical and electronic equipment.

## Scope
- All EEE categories (household, IT, lighting, consumer electronics)
- Medical devices and monitoring equipment with transitional periods
- Exemptions listed in Annex III/IV (time-limited)

## When it applies
Whenever the product is EEE. Spare parts and sub-assemblies are included.

## Obligations by role
- **Manufacturer:** Evaluate materials and processes, maintain technical documentation, issue DoC, ensure CE mark (through sector directives)
- **Importer:** Confirm manufacturer compliance and keep documentation
- **EU authorised representative:** Provide DoC and liaise with authorities

## Conformity assessment
Internal production control based on supply-chain risk assessment, material analysis and, where necessary, laboratory testing.

## Documentation
- Technical documentation per EN IEC 63000
- Supplier declarations/material disclosures (e.g. IPC-1752A)
- Bills of material, substance assessments, test reports

## Marking
No dedicated symbol; CE marking presumes RoHS conformity.

## Retention & traceability
Maintain records for 10 years. Track materials to supplier/batch level.

## Note
Monitor delegated directives for new restricted substances and national notification duties (e.g. ECHA SCIP).
