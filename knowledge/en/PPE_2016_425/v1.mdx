# PPE Regulation (EU) 2016/425

## Meaning
Sets requirements for placing personal protective equipment on the market to protect users against health and safety risks.

## Scope
- Protective clothing, helmets, gloves, respiratory and fall protection, etc.
- Excludes equipment for armed forces and PPE made for personal use without commercialisation

## When it applies
Whenever a product is designed to protect individuals from risks and is made available on the market.

## Obligations by role
- **Manufacturer:** Risk assessment, technical documentation, CE marking, conformity assessment per category
- **Importer:** Ensure conformity documentation exists and storage conditions maintain performance
- **EU authorised representative:** Hold technical file and support authorities

## Conformity assessment
- Category I: Module A
- Category II: Module B + C or D
- Category III: Module B + C2 or D with ongoing surveillance by a notified body

## Documentation
- Risk assessment, test reports per relevant EN standards
- Instructions for use in the language of the Member State
- EU Declaration of Conformity

## Marking
- CE mark; Category III products show notified body number
- Manufacturer/importer identification
- Pictograms and performance levels

## Retention & traceability
Maintain technical file and declaration for 10 years. Keep batch identifiers and quality management evidence.

## Note
Where PPE is simultaneously a medical device (e.g. surgical masks) MDR requirements also apply.
