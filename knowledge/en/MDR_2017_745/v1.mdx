# Medical Device Regulation (EU) 2017/745

## Meaning
Governs placing medical devices on the EU market, reinforcing clinical evidence, vigilance and traceability through the UDI system.

## Scope
- Medical devices and accessories, software with a medical purpose
- Products without medical purpose listed in Annex XVI (e.g. cosmetic contact lenses)

## When it applies
If the product has a medical purpose as defined in Article 2 or falls within Annex XVI.

## Obligations by role
- **Manufacturer:** Implement QMS, conduct clinical evaluation, compile technical documentation, run post-market surveillance, register in EUDAMED
- **Importer:** Verify CE, UDI and DoC, ensure traceability
- **EU authorised representative:** Maintain mandate, hold documentation, liaise with authorities

## Conformity assessment
Depends on risk class I–III. Higher classes require notified body involvement and audits (Annex IX–XI).

## Documentation
- Technical documentation per Annex II & III
- Clinical evaluation and PMCF plans
- Risk management (EN ISO 14971), instructions for use

## Marking
- CE mark (with NB number where applicable)
- UDI-DI and UDI-PI on product, packaging and labels
- Economic operator details in required languages

## Retention & traceability
Keep documentation for 10 years (15 for implantables). Maintain UDI data in EUDAMED and track serial/batch numbers.

## Note
Observe transition deadlines through 2027/2028; legacy devices need MDR conformity before expiry.
