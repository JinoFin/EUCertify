# Battery Regulation (EU) 2023/1542

## Meaning
Replaces the Battery Directive with directly applicable lifecycle obligations covering sustainability, labelling and due diligence for all battery types.

## Scope
- Portable, industrial, automotive and LMT batteries
- Embedded and replaceable batteries in devices
- Producers, importers, authorised representatives and re-manufacturers

## When it applies
Whenever a product contains a battery or the battery itself is placed on the market. Check phased deadlines for sustainability and due diligence requirements.

## Obligations by role
- **Manufacturer/Importer:** Provide sustainability labelling, QR codes/battery passport, carbon footprint, implement supply-chain due diligence
- **EU authorised representative:** Handle registration, documentation and market surveillance interactions

## Conformity assessment
Requires EU Declaration of Conformity, potential third-party verification for recycling targets, and robust environmental management systems.

## Documentation
- Technical files (composition, performance)
- Evidence of collection and recycling targets
- Due diligence and sustainability reports

## Marking
- QR code linking to battery passport data
- Capacity information and pictograms (e.g. crossed-out wheeled bin, chemical symbols)

## Retention & traceability
Maintain supply-chain, collection and carbon data for 10 years. Assign serial/batch references in the battery passport.

## Note
Recycled content thresholds apply from 2027; collection targets tighten from 2025 onward.
