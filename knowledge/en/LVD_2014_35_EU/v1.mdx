# LVD 2014/35/EU – Low Voltage Directive

## Meaning
Sets essential safety requirements for electrical equipment between 50–1000 VAC and 75–1500 VDC, protecting people and property from electrical hazards.

## Scope
- Mass-produced and custom electrical equipment within the voltage range
- Power supplies, household appliances, tools, IT and AV gear
- Excludes ATEX equipment, medical devices (MDR) and radio equipment covered by RED

## When it applies
Whenever the product is powered by mains voltage or comparable DC levels. Battery products are included when supplied with a charger operating in the voltage window.

## Obligations by role
- **Manufacturer:** Risk assessment, technical file, test reports, EU Declaration of Conformity, CE marking
- **Importer:** Verify documentation, ensure CE and labelling requirements, keep copies available
- **EU authorised representative:** Hold DoC and cooperate with market surveillance

## Conformity assessment
Normally Module A (internal production control). Engage a notified body if relying on non-harmonised safety standards.

## Documentation
- Hazard and risk assessment
- Bills of materials, schematics, layouts
- Test reports (e.g. EN 62368-1, EN 60335-1)
- EU Declaration of Conformity referencing the LVD

## Marking
- CE mark
- Manufacturer/importer name and address
- Rating plate with electrical parameters and warnings where relevant

## Retention & traceability
Keep the technical file and declaration for 10 years after last product placement. Record serial numbers and production batches.

## Additional notes
Consider mechanical, thermal and acoustic hazards linked to electrical design.
