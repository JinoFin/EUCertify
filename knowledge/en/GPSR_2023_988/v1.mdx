# GPSR (EU) 2023/988 – General Product Safety Regulation

## Meaning
Directly applicable from 13 December 2024, replacing the GPSD and modernising consumer product safety obligations, including digital and online aspects.

## Scope
- Consumer products lacking sector-specific EU legislation
- Online marketplaces, fulfilment service providers and repairers have explicit duties

## When it applies
Whenever a product is intended for consumers or can reasonably be used by them, in addition to sector legislation (e.g. LVD, RED).

## Obligations by role
- **Manufacturer:** Conduct safety assessment, create product safety file, prepare recall and incident management processes, notify via Safety Gate
- **Importer:** Ensure manufacturer obligations are met, designate an EU contact point
- **EU authorised representative:** Act as market surveillance liaison and report serious incidents

## Conformity assessment
No modules; requires documented risk assessment, evidence of tests and an effective market surveillance plan.

## Documentation
- Safety assessment covering foreseeable misuse
- Recall plan and communication templates
- Product monitoring records and incident logs

## Marking
- Name/contact of the responsible economic operator on the product
- Digital instructions/QR codes recommended for traceability

## Retention & traceability
Keep safety assessments, recall records and incident data for 10 years. Track serial numbers and distribution channels.

## Note
Online marketplaces must implement Know Your Business Customer checks—provide machine-readable compliance data.
