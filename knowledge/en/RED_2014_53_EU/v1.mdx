# RED 2014/53/EU – Radio Equipment Directive

## Meaning
Combines safety, EMC and spectrum requirements for radio equipment to ensure efficient use of the radio spectrum across the EU.

## Scope
- Radio modules and end products using Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, cellular, SRD, UWB
- Combined products with radio and non-radio functions
- Excludes amateur radio gear not placed on the market, marine equipment, aeronautical radio

## When it applies
As soon as a product transmits radio signals or integrates a radio module. RED replaces LVD and EMC obligations for those devices but the underlying safety/EMC evidence remains mandatory.

## Obligations by role
- **Manufacturer:** Provide radio test reports (e.g. EN 300 328), spectrum and spurious emissions evidence, technical documentation, CE and, where needed, class identifiers
- **Importer:** Ensure frequency information and warnings are available in the destination language
- **EU authorised representative:** Retain conformity documents and support authorities

## Conformity assessment
Module A when harmonised standards are fully applied. Otherwise apply Module B+C or Module H with a notified body.

## Documentation
- EU Declaration of Conformity (Annex VI model)
- Radio test reports (transmit power, spectrum, receiver performance)
- EMC and electrical safety reports
- Frequency usage statement

## Marking
- CE mark and notified body number if used
- Radio class identifiers where required
- Country/frequency restriction notices

## Retention & traceability
Keep records for 10 years, link serial numbers to radio modules and firmware versions. Document software update control.

## Note
Provide interface descriptions for open platforms as required by Article 3(3).
