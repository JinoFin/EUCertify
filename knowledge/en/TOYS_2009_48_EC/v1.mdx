# Toy Safety Directive 2009/48/EC

## Meaning
Defines safety requirements for toys intended for children under 14 years of age.

## Scope
- Toys, construction sets, educational toys, connected toys
- Excludes collectibles, sports equipment, decorative items

## When it applies
If the product is marketed as a toy or is likely to be perceived as such by children.

## Obligations by role
- **Manufacturer:** Perform safety assessment (mechanical, chemical, electrical), follow conformity assessment, apply CE mark, provide warnings
- **Importer:** Verify manufacturer obligations and ensure warnings appear in the target language
- **EU authorised representative:** Maintain documentation and support authorities

## Conformity assessment
Module B+C or Module H, depending on product risk. Third-party testing is often required.

## Documentation
- Safety assessments, test reports (EN 71 series, EN 62115)
- Warnings and age grading
- EU Declaration of Conformity

## Marking
- CE mark, manufacturer/importer address
- Age grading, warning pictograms, specific hazard statements (e.g. “Not suitable for children under 36 months”)

## Retention & traceability
Keep documents for 10 years. Record batch/serial numbers and production sites.

## Note
Connected toys with radio features must also comply with RED and data protection requirements.
